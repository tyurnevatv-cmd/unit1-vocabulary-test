VOCABULARY CHECK90-second warm-up

Choose the right verb

Meaning matters in news English.

LAUNCH

Start a drone or missile flight.
launch a drone attack

STRIKE / HIT

Strike = attack a target.
Hit = actually make contact with it.

INTERCEPT / SHOOT DOWN

Intercept = engage an incoming threat.
Shoot down = bring it down.

Say it aloud: Which verb fits? (1) ______ a missile; (2) ______ a target; (3) ______ an incoming drone. Give the most natural option and explain why.
Show answers
1. launch a missile; 2. strike / hit a target (with different meanings); 3. intercept / shoot down an incoming drone (with different meanings).

GRAMMAR • REPORTED SPEECHBackshift after a past reporting verb

What changes in indirect speech?

The reporter said that… / The expert explained that…

Present Simple → Past Simple
“Drones travel fast.” → The reporter said drones travelled fast.
Present Continuous → Past Continuous
“They are tracking the drone.” → The crew said they were tracking it.
Present Perfect → Past Perfect
“Production has increased.” → The report said it had increased.
Past Simple → Past Perfect
“They launched the missile.” → She said they had launched it.
will → would
“We will scale up production.” → They said they would scale up production.
can → could
“The drone can fly higher.” → He said the drone could fly higher.
Time expressions also change when needed: last night → the night before; tomorrow → the following day. Backshift is not always necessary if the fact is still true.

WRITING • REPORTED SPEECHTransform and vary reporting verbs

Advanced news reporting

Use noted / explained / stated / warned / added where appropriate.

C2
5 points • tense, passive, conditional, future perfect
  1. “We have been developing interceptor drones for several months.”
  2. “The new drones are being produced on a larger scale.”
  3. “If interceptor production increases, air defence will improve.”
  4. “Several UAVs were intercepted before they reached the target.”
  5. “By the end of next year, we will have expanded production.”
Show answers
  1. The spokesperson stated that they had been developing interceptor drones for several months.
  2. The report noted that the new drones were being produced on a larger scale.
  3. The analyst explained that if interceptor production increased, air defence would improve.
  4. The official stated that several UAVs had been intercepted before they reached the target.
  5. The company announced that by the end of the following year, it would have expanded production.
