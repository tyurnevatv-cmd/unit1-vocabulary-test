MILITARY ENGLISH · LESSON 1

1981 vs 2026 · What has changed?

Original textbook (1981)

Department of Defense (DoD) – министерство обороны

Secretary of Defense (SECDEF) – министр обороны

Five armed services appear in the list: Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps and Coast Guard.

Current official usage (2026)

Department of War (DoW) – министерство войны (дополнительное официальное название)

Secretary of War (SECWAR) – министр войны (дополнительный титул)

The Space Force was established in 2019; the United States now has six military services.

Important: “Department of Defense” remains the legal name under federal law. “Department of War” is an authorised secondary title. The Army, Navy and Air Force departments have not all been renamed.

Sources: Executive Order 14347; U.S. Department of War – Our Forces.
