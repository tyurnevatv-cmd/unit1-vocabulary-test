Terminology · Then and now

In Nelyubin (1981)

Department of Defense (DoD)
Secretary of Defense (SECDEF)

Five service branches in the list.

Current usage (2026)

Department of War (DoW)
Secretary of War (SECWAR)

Space Force is the sixth branch.

Under U.S. law, Department of Defense remains the statutory name. Department of War is an authorised secondary designation, not a renaming of every military department.
