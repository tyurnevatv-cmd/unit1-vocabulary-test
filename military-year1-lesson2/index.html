THERE IS / THERE ARE

Study the rules and examples.

CADET · A1–A2

Introduce something new

THERE + BE + NOUN (+ PLACE / TIME)

There is an Army representative at the council.
There are two chiefs of staff in the room.
There is a Navy meeting today.

Singular / uncountable: there is · there was
Plural: there are · there were
Future: there will be

Ask and make negative statements

Is there a meeting at the Department of Defense?
Are there any new defence plans?
There isn’t a Coast Guard office here.
There aren’t any chairs in the room.

Compare: There is a briefing in the hall (new information) vs The briefing is in the hall (its location is already known).

Reference: Drozdova et al., English Grammar, § 2, pp. 11–13.

ADVANCED: THERE IS / THERE ARE

Special cases of there is / there are.

1. Agreement in lists

There was a council meeting, two Navy representatives and a chief of staff. There were two Navy representatives, a council meeting and a chief of staff.

In formal edited English, agreement normally follows the first item. Informal there's + plural occurs, but avoid it in a translation test.

2. Modals & reporting

There may be a delay. There seems to be a misunderstanding. There arose a disagreement.

The existential construction is not limited to is/are.

3. There being...

There being no new instructions, the Army remained in position.

Equivalent in meaning to Because there were no new orders...; a compact way to express a reason.

4. It or there?

It is essential to verify the security information. There is no point in repeating the same error. It's no secret that the council meeting was cancelled.

Compare introductory it and existential there; some expressions are fixed.
