CADET · A1–A2

TO BE

am · is · are

Identity, description and location.

The Air Force is a military service.
The armed forces are ready.
Intelligence is important.

I am / he, she, it is / we, you, they are. Remember: uncountable intelligence + is.

OFFICER · B2–C1

BE TO + INFINITIVE

is to · are to · was to

Formal orders, plans, prohibition and passive instructions.

The reserve component is to report.
Personnel are not to leave.
Intelligence is to be protected.

Be to ≠ be going to. It often signals an official order or arrangement.
